# G Inc Vendor and Processor Management Standard

**Document class:** Synthetic company standard draft — not law  
**Company:** G Inc (fictional)  
**Version:** 0.1 (demo draft)  
**Owner:** Fictional Procurement Lead and Privacy Lead  
**Review date:** 2026-11-01  
**Approval status:** Unapproved; created only for software demonstration

> This draft is an internal control proposal. It does not determine the legal status of a vendor or make a legal-compliance claim.

## 1. Before onboarding

The business sponsor must document the service, purpose, personal-data categories, affected groups, access path, processing locations known to G Inc, and proposed retention. Procurement and the fictional Privacy Lead should review the vendor’s security and privacy materials, incident process, subcontractors, and deletion capabilities before access is granted. Higher-risk services require counsel and Security Lead review.

## 2. Written terms and instructions

Before the vendor handles personal data, an authorized G Inc owner should ensure a written agreement defines the service and permitted processing, confidentiality, appropriate security commitments, incident escalation, support for individual requests where relevant, subcontractor controls, return or deletion at termination, and cooperation with lawful audit or investigation requirements. The approved agreement and instructions must be stored in the contract repository.

## 3. Access and changes

Vendor access must be limited to named accounts and the minimum permissions needed. The sponsor must approve access, set an end date, and arrange prompt revocation when the work ends. Material changes to purpose, data categories, subprocessors, or processing locations require review before the change is accepted.

## 4. Incidents and exit

The vendor must use the contact and escalation path specified in its approved agreement to report a suspected incident involving G Inc data. G Inc’s Security and Privacy Leads assess the report under the incident response procedure. At exit, the sponsor obtains confirmation of return or deletion as provided in the contract, terminates credentials, and records any approved retention exception.

## 5. Review cycle

The sponsor reviews the vendor relationship at least annually in this demo standard and after a material security or service change. This interval is an invented internal control target, not a legal requirement. Procurement records the review date, decision, unresolved risks, owner, and remediation due date.
