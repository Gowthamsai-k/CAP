# G Inc Personal Data Incident Response Procedure

**Document class:** Synthetic company procedure draft — not law  
**Company:** G Inc (fictional)  
**Version:** 0.1 (demo draft)  
**Owner:** Fictional Security Lead and Privacy Lead  
**Review date:** 2026-11-01  
**Approval status:** Unapproved; created only for software demonstration

> Response targets below are internal demo targets, not statutory reporting deadlines. Counsel must determine any applicable external notice or regulator reporting requirements using the law and commencement status in force for the incident.

## 1. Report and record

Any employee or contractor who suspects unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure of personal data should contact the fictional Security Lead immediately through the internal incident channel. The on-call lead should open an incident record and notify the Privacy Lead within one hour as an internal response target. Record known facts and uncertainties; do not speculate about affected people or legal consequences.

## 2. Triage and containment

The Security Lead coordinates technical triage: identify affected systems and data categories, preserve logs and relevant evidence, restrict compromised credentials or access, and contain the incident without destroying evidence. System owners should support containment and provide a timeline. Do not make public statements or contact affected individuals without approval from the authorized incident lead and counsel.

## 3. Assess impact and obligations

The Privacy Lead, Security Lead, business owner, and counsel should assess what happened, when it was discovered, what personal data and individuals may be affected, whether a service provider is involved, and what corrective actions are needed. Counsel determines whether any notice to individuals, regulators, customers, or other parties is required and by what deadline under the applicable law and commencement provisions. The incident team must record the sources and assumptions used for that determination.

## 4. Communicate and remediate

Only the designated incident spokesperson may issue approved communications. The response team should document containment, recovery, and measures to prevent recurrence. A post-incident review should identify root cause, control gaps, owners, and due dates. Track corrective actions to closure.

## 5. Close and retain records

The incident lead may close the incident after evidence is preserved, required decisions and communications are recorded, systems are recovered, and corrective actions have owners. Retain the incident file under the approved records schedule or legal hold. Limit access to people who need it for response, investigation, or legal review.
