# G Inc Employee and Customer Privacy Policy

**Document class:** Synthetic company policy draft — not law  
**Company:** G Inc (fictional)  
**Jurisdictional scenario:** India  
**Version:** 0.1 (demo draft)  
**Owner:** Fictional Privacy Lead  
**Review date:** 2026-11-01  
**Approval status:** Unapproved; created only for software demonstration

> This document is invented for a demo. It is not legal advice, proof of compliance, or an approved policy. It intentionally contains operational commitments that G Inc would need to validate and implement.

## 1. Purpose and scope

This draft describes how fictional G Inc proposes to handle personal data in its Indian software-services business. It covers website and product users, prospective and current customers, job applicants, employees, contractors, and business contacts. It is a sample internal policy, not a substitute for a notice tailored to each product or processing purpose.

## 2. Data categories and purposes

Depending on the relationship and service, G Inc may handle:

- account identifiers and contact details;
- support requests, product usage, and service diagnostics;
- billing and business-contact information;
- recruitment records and employment administration information; and
- access, security, and audit records.

Before a product or team collects data, its owner must document the specific purpose, data fields, collection channel, systems, recipients, and retention rationale. Teams must not reuse information for a materially different purpose without review by the fictional Privacy Lead.

## 3. Collection and transparency

Product owners must provide a clear, standalone privacy notice for each collection context. The notice should explain the data collected, the purposes, how an individual can contact G Inc, and the available process for requests or complaints. Product teams must collect only the fields needed for the documented purpose and must not make optional fields appear mandatory.

## 4. Access and use

Personal data may be accessed only by personnel who need it for an assigned business task. Staff must use approved systems, protect credentials, and avoid copying personal data to personal devices, public repositories, or unapproved collaboration tools. Managers must review access when a role changes or ends.

## 5. Requests and questions

Individuals may contact `privacy@g-inc.example` (a fictional, non-routable demo address) to ask a question, make a request, or raise a concern. The support team should log the request and route it to the fictional Privacy Lead within two business days as an internal service target. That target is an invented company goal, not a statement of a legal response deadline. The Privacy Lead should verify identity proportionately, determine what records are relevant, coordinate with system owners, document the response, and seek counsel where the applicable legal position is uncertain.

## 6. Sharing and service providers

G Inc should disclose personal data to a service provider only for a documented business purpose, under written instructions and an approved agreement. The business owner must record the provider, data involved, purpose, access, and deletion/return expectations. Requests from public authorities must be escalated to counsel and the Privacy Lead before a response, except where immediate action is legally required.

## 7. Security, incidents, and retention

G Inc should apply access controls, encryption appropriate to the data and system, secure development practices, logging, backups, and incident reporting. Suspected loss, unauthorized access, or disclosure must be reported using the incident procedure. Retention and deletion follow the separate G Inc retention standard and any documented legal hold.

## 8. Ownership and review

The fictional Privacy Lead owns this draft. Product and HR owners are responsible for implementing approved procedures in their systems. This draft should be reviewed when the company changes a product, data use, vendor, or relevant legal requirements. No provision is approved until G Inc’s actual decision-makers and counsel review it.
