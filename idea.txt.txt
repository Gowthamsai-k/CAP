"An AI platform that helps multinational companies stay legally compliant across different countries using LLMs and RAG."
What it does
🌍 Explains country-specific laws and regulations.
🤖 Answers compliance questions using official documents.
📊 Compares regulations between countries.
📄 Reviews contracts for compliance issues.
⚠️ Identifies compliance risks before business expansion.
🔔 Alerts users when important regulations change.
Who can use it?
Multinational companies
Startups expanding internationally
HR teams
Finance teams
Legal teams
Compliance officers
The core idea is not to replace lawyers, but to help businesses find and understand compliance information faster and with references to official sources.
